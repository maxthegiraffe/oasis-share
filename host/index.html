How to Connect

Step 1: Get Your Connector Link

Sign in to app.grateful.gg and open your event's host panel, then the Event tab. Click "Make my Claude link". Your private link is shown once — save it somewhere safe. (You can revoke it anytime with "Turn it off".)

Step 2: Add to Claude

In Claude, go to Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Paste your link and name it "Grateful Giraffes".

Step 3: Start Creating

Start a chat and ask Claude about your events. For example: "What events do I host?" Then describe what you want to build or change.
