06 · Story

Every morning, three sentences.

Every morning, our founder Max Marine says three sentences.

I forgive. I thank. I love.

We make quiet clothes for living in, in the fibers you would choose if you could see the numbers: loopback cotton terry, fine cotton jersey, Grade A cashmere. Outside there is nothing to read, only one ochre stitch at the hem. It goes through the cloth. Inside, where it lands, are those three sentences and one open line that says For.

The Grateful Giraffes, the community this came from, say a giraffe lives with its head in the clouds, its feet on the ground and a big heart. So every top and bottom comes in Regular and Tall, and our wrap is six feet long, the length of a giraffe's neck.

And every order carries an unwritten card, so you can thank someone else.

Wear your gratitude on the inside.
