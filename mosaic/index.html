PER PORTAM IRIDIS

Through the rainbow gate. The Romans called the giraffe camelopardalis, a camel with a leopard's spots. Each tessera was placed one at a time by Claude Code: the figure, the floor in straight courses, the sawtooth border, the inscription, then the rainbow road and the portal. The stones of the portal never move; only their colours turn. As the giraffe nears the gate its spots go rainbow, nose first. After PELECANVS IN BIROTA by @redp314.
