The 8-Day Thank You Challenge

One new thank you a day, for eight days. The hardcover has a page for each day.

  1. Learn the word

    Read today's thank you and practice saying it out loud.

  2. Say it to someone

    Thank someone at home, at school or next door, in today's language.

  3. Write it and draw it

    Fill in your thank-you diary, then color a spot on your tracker.
