04How it works

You bring a question.
We ask the herd.

  1. 01

    You bring a question

    Tell us what you are trying to learn and who you need to hear from.

  2. 02

    We ask the herd

    Together we pick the panel and the format and shape the questions. Members answer when they check in with Gigi, or we ask in the room. Partner questions are always clearly marked, and the panel hears who is asking before any results go out.

  3. 03

    You get the pattern

    What the group said, how strongly and where it splits, with short quotes cleared under the anonymization rules we set with the founding panel. Never a name, never one person's full answers.

Zoom in as far as you like. A spot has no name on it.

05Our commitments

Patterns, never people.

We wrote these down before signing a single partner.

  1. i.
    You see patterns, not people

    Results come pooled across the group. You never get a name, a phone number, an email or one person's answers.

  2. ii.
    Members opt in

    Partner questions only go to Giraffes who say yes to the panel. Nothing a member told us before saying yes goes to partners. Only answers given after joining the panel are ever pooled for a partner.

  3. iii.
    The panel hears who you are

    Before any partner sees results, the founding panel hears who they are. How a member sits out a partner gets set with the panel before the first one signs.

  4. iv.
    Groups big enough to hide in

    We only report on groups large enough that no one stands out, and we publish the minimum group size before the first report.

  5. v.
    Half goes to the members who answer

    Half of what you pay will go to the founding panel members who take the Pulse during your partnership, as pay for their time. Timing and payout method get set with the panel before the first payout. The other half funds the gatherings, tools and programs Grateful Labs builds for the community.
