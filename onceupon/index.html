The heart behind the book

Gratitude is for anyone, and within everyone. Being grateful is not just good for you, but for everyone around you. Every moment is a gift, and the more we can remember that, the more we get out of life.

Written by Max Marine and made by the Grateful Giraffes, a community of people who practice gratitude together. Giraffes have the biggest hearts of any land animal, and they keep their heads in the clouds and their feet on the ground. That is how we try to live too.

Why gratitude? In a study of middle schoolers, counting blessings each day was linked to more satisfaction with school and fewer bad moods.Froh, Sefick and Emmons, Journal of School Psychology, 2008

Questions, answered

Who is it for?

Children ages 3 to 8, and anyone who loves reading aloud with them. The words are short enough to sing and the pictures carry the big ideas.

How does the song work?

Point a phone camera at the code inside the book and the song plays, so kids can sing along as the pages turn. You can also play it right here on this page.

Paperback or Kindle?

The paperback is 6 x 9 inches with 46 full-color pages. The Kindle edition shows every page exactly as printed, on a phone, a tablet or a Kindle.

Can I order from outside the US?

Yes. The buttons on this page open the Amazon store for your country, including the UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Spain, Italy and Japan. You can switch stores at the bottom of the page.

What's the story about?

How we came to be, from the birth of the universe to the magic of everyday life: the first stars, the first green leaf, the dinosaurs, the first people, all the way to today. Along the way, children around the world say thank you.
