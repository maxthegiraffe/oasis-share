From the author

Why I wrote this book

Hi, I'm Max. 🦒

Four years ago, we started the Grateful Giraffes with one mission: to make the world a more grateful place. We've gathered 500 times since then, and every gathering taught me the same thing. When people say thank you out loud, they soften, they open up, and they come together.

Then I thought about the kids. They're the future, and what they practice now becomes the world they build. A child who learns to say thank you for the stars, the water and the people who love them grows up seeing the gifts all around them.

So I wrote a song, Once Upon a Thank You. Then we illustrated it, and now it's a book, live on Amazon. It tells the story of how we came to be, from the first stars to the people we love. Along the way, families around the world say thank you in eight languages. Every page is a line of the song, so you can read it and sing it together.

My hope is simple: that families everywhere make thank you a daily song, and that a generation of grateful kids changes the world.

Head in the clouds, feet on the ground, big hearts.
Max Marine
