How we ran the numbers

Same model for every building

  • Rent: a triple-net master lease to Grateful Giraffes at $4.25 per square foot a month, rising 3% a year, starting in year 2 with three months free.
  • Retrofit: construction priced per square foot by starting condition (church or hall $79, office $55, mixed-use $70, flex $95, warehouse $110, calibrated on 132 Brooks), plus 26.7% for design and permits and a 10% contingency.
  • Financing: a bank loan of about 50% of total cost at 7.0% fixed, 25-year amortization; a reserve covers 12 months of mortgage while the retrofit runs; 2% closing costs; $100,000 for formation.
  • Investors: an 8% cumulative preferred return, then 75/25. No sale, refinance, appreciation or studio value is counted.
  • Operator plan scaled to each building's size from the 132 Brooks plan. Listings change daily: confirm status and price with the broker before any offer.
