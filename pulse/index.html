Your privacy, in plain words

Your answers are yours. Partners only see patterns.

  1. i.
    Never your name, number or email

    Partners will never get your name, phone number or email. How answers get anonymized will be written up and shown to the founding panel before anything leaves Grateful Labs.

  2. ii.
    Patterns, never people

    Partners will only see results pooled across many Giraffes, never one person’s answers. The minimum group size gets set with the founding panel before anything is shared.

  3. iii.
    You hear about partners first

    Before any partner sees results, we’ll tell the founding panel who they are. How you sit out a partner you’re not comfortable with gets decided with the panel before the first one signs.

  4. iv.
    Only what you share after yes

    Nothing you’ve already told Gigi goes to partners. Those answers stay on your profile to shape your invitations. Only Pulse answers you give after saying yes would ever be pooled. Want out? Tell Max or Julian and you’re off.

Questions

Before you join.

How do I get paid?

When a partner pays, half of that payment goes to the founding panel members who took the Pulse during that partnership, weighted by how consistently they showed up. Timing and payout method get set with the founding panel before the first payout. No partner has signed yet, so nothing has been paid out so far.

How long does it take?

Five minutes a week, minimum. Go deeper whenever you want. Once it opens, text when it suits you, or call if you’d rather talk.

What does Gigi ask?

Right now, when a Giraffe texts or calls Gigi, she asks a one-time set of questions so we can tailor your invitations. The weekly Pulse questions are new, and we’re shaping them with the founding panel. If a partner ever wanted to add a question, it would always be clearly marked.

What happens when I text “Pulse”?

Gigi passes your message to Max, so he knows you want a seat on the founding panel. If you’ve never done Gigi’s one-time questions, she may start those first. The founding panel is what starts the weekly check-in, and we’ll share the full details, privacy rules and payouts included, before the first weekly Pulse.

Who can join?

Grateful Giraffes. If Gigi doesn’t recognize your number, text Max or Julian and they’ll add it. Not a Giraffe yet? Apply at grateful.gg/apply.

Can I skip a week or stop?

Yes. Skip any week. How streaks count gets set with the founding panel before the first payout. To leave the panel, tell Max or Julian and you’re off.
