Human Design · strategy and authority

Deciding together

Alexa knows in her gut, in the moment. Max finds out what he thinks by hearing himself say it. Those fit well, in this order.

  1. Max asks, Alexa responds.Yes or no questions work best. “Do you want to do this?” lands better than “What should we do?”
  2. Alexa answers with the sound first.An uh-huh or an un-un from the gut is her real answer. The reasons can come later, or never.
  3. Max talks it out.He needs someone to listen while he thinks out loud, without advice. Alexa listening, not steering, is how he hears his own yes.
  4. Nobody pitches.Max's insight works when it has been invited. Alexa's energy works when it is responding. A pitch skips both.

The signals: bitterness in Max and frustration in Alexa. When either shows up, the usual cause is guidance nobody asked for, or a yes her gut never gave. Success for him and satisfaction for her mean it is working.
